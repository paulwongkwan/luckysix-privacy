Privacy Policy — Lucky Six

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · App: Lucky Six (com.mememe.luckysix) · Contact: paulwongkwan@gmail.com

The short version

Lucky Six does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps your most recent 50 generated lines (the numbers, the time and which source produced them) in its own private storage. That data:

  • never leaves your device through the app;
  • is deleted when you clear the history in the app or uninstall it;
  • may be included in an Android system backup if you have device backup enabled. That backup is handled by Google or your device manufacturer under their own privacy policies, not by us.

Network access

The app has one network feature: to produce a line, it asks random.org for a randomly ordered list of the numbers 1 to 49. The request contains only that number range — no account, no identifier and nothing about you or your device. If the request fails, the app generates the numbers locally and no further request is made.

random.org is an independent service with its own privacy policy, available at https://www.random.org/privacy/.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, advertising ID or ad SDKs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or attribution SDKs.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.

Permissions

The app requests only the INTERNET permission, used for the random.org request described above. It declares no runtime permissions, so Android never asks you to approve access to anything.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete all locally stored lines at any time with History → Clear, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
