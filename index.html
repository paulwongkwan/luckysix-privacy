Privacy Policy — Lucky Six

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · App: Lucky Six (com.mememe.luckysix) · Version 1.1.0 and later · Contact: paulwongkwan@gmail.com

The short version

Lucky Six has no accounts and does not collect your lottery picks. Your saved lines stay on your device. The app shows a banner ad supplied by Google AdMob, which processes limited device and ad-interaction data as described below.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps your most recent 50 generated lines (the numbers, the time and which source produced them) in its own private storage. That data:

  • never leaves your device through the app;
  • is deleted when you clear the history in the app or uninstall it;
  • may be included in an Android system backup if you have device backup enabled. That backup is handled by Google or your device manufacturer under their own privacy policies, not by us.

Network access

The app has one network feature: to produce a line, it asks random.org for a randomly ordered list of the numbers 1 to 49. The request contains only that number range — no account, no identifier and nothing about you or your device. If the request fails, the app generates the numbers locally and no further request is made.

random.org is an independent service with its own privacy policy, available at https://www.random.org/privacy/.

Advertising

The main screen shows one banner ad supplied by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To request, display and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be inferred;
  • information about your interactions with the ad, such as impressions and clicks;
  • diagnostic information about the ad SDK.

This information is shared with Google and used for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. We do not receive it, store it on a server, or combine it with your generated lines.

You can learn more and control ad personalisation here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites
  • Ad Settings (opt out of personalised ads)
  • On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or attribution SDKs of our own.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No selling of your data, and no sharing except with Google for the ad serving described above.

Permissions

The app requests only internet access and network-state access, used for the random.org request and for ads. It declares no runtime permissions, so Android never asks you to approve access to anything.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and the ads are not intended for children.

Your choices

You can delete all locally stored lines at any time with History → Clear, or by uninstalling the app. Advertising personalisation can be limited with the Google Ad Settings link above or your device's advertising-ID controls.

Changes
